PlayChamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · App: PlayChamp - Sports Academy (com.playchamp.app)

PlayChamp is a mobile app that connects sports academies, coaches, players, parents, turf owners and specialists such as dietitians and physiotherapists. It is operated by Ethimo Private Limited, No 67, Vembuliamman Koil St, OMR, Padur, Chennai – 603103, Tamil Nadu, India ("PlayChamp", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

1. Data we collect

WhatDetails
Account dataName, email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. We never see or store it in readable form.
Profile dataYour role (academy owner, coach, student or player, parent, turf owner), sports, short bio, and any photo you choose to upload.
Academy and training dataAcademy name, city and phone, batches, training sessions, attendance (present or absent for each session, who marked it and when, and the short reason a parent gives when a child cannot attend), fee records and their paid or due status, announcements, and progress notes entered by coaches or academies.
Children's dataName, sport, batch, attendance, fees and progress of a child (a person under 18) that a parent, guardian or academy adds. See section 7.
Performance resultsResults a coach records for a student, such as scores, times, distances and match statistics, with the date and the event. The student, their linked parents and the academy staff can see them.
Coach feedbackNotes, and optional skill ratings from 1 to 5, that the owner, the head coach or a coach writes about a student. A parent or student sees a note only after the coach shares it. Drafts stay with the academy staff.
EquipmentItems a student uses or is advised to buy (item, brand, size, condition, date bought and upgrade date), added by a parent, a coach or the owner.
Bookings and requestsTurf and court bookings (venue, date, time, price), session requests to coaches and specialists, requests to join an academy, and care or plan entries shared by specialists.
Ratings and reviewsRatings and comments you give to academies, coaches or specialists.
AreaThe city and locality you pick in the app to find places near you. We do not use GPS and do not track your live location.
Technical dataIP address, device and app version, and error or security logs created by our hosting and authentication provider when you use the app.

We do not collect your contacts, SMS, precise location, financial account or card numbers, or government identity numbers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics SDK.

2. Why we use your data

  • To create your account, sign you in and reset your password.
  • To run the features you use: academy management, attendance (a parent or student marks present and the coach approves it), fees tracking, bookings, discovery of nearby academies, coaches, turfs and specialists, ratings, and notifications.
  • To send service emails, such as the password reset code and, when you join, a welcome message. We do not send marketing email without asking you.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix bugs and meet legal obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent (you give it when you sign up and use the app), to deliver the service you asked for, and to comply with law.

3. Who can see your data

  • Academies and coaches you join. The owner of an academy you join can see your name, attendance, fees, feedback and equipment. The head coach and the coaches assigned to you can see your attendance, feedback and equipment, but not your fees.
  • Parents and guardians. A parent linked to a child can see that child's attendance, fees, equipment and the feedback the coach shared, and can add equipment records.
  • Specialists you book. A dietitian, physiotherapist or personal coach you book can see the details you share with them for that booking.
  • Turf owners see your name and booking details for bookings at their venue.
  • The public can see what an academy, coach, turf or specialist chooses to list (name, city, sports, ratings) in the app's discovery screens.
  • Our service providers (section 4), only to run the app for us.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account (section 8), we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days. We may keep limited records longer only where the law requires, for example fee or booking records needed for accounts, or to resolve a dispute. Backups are overwritten on our normal schedule.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted by row-level security rules in our database, so a user can read only the records they are allowed to see. Passwords are stored as hashes. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Children

PlayChamp is not directed at children. Many students are under 18, so accounts and records for a person under 18 must be created or approved by a parent, legal guardian or an academy that has the guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect data directly from a child without that consent. We do not show advertising to anyone and we do not track or profile children for marketing. A parent or guardian can ask us to see, correct or delete a child's data at any time using the contact details below.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you,
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete (most of it you can edit in the app),
  • delete your account and personal data,
  • withdraw your consent, which may mean we can no longer provide the service, and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you cannot.

To delete your account in the app: open Profile, scroll to the end and tap "Delete my account". We delete your login and personal details (name, email, phone, photo) within 30 days. You can cancel the request in the same place until then. If you cannot open the app, or you have already removed it, use the email option below.

To delete your account by email: email ethimotechnologies@ethimo.in from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my PlayChamp account". We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days. If you are an academy owner, tell us whether to delete the academy's data as well, because other members' records belong to the academy.

We may update this policy. If we make a significant change we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
